# Privacy policy for Ben's Steps

Last updated: [date]

Ben's Steps helps students break schoolwork into small steps. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: your things stay on your device.

**Who we are.** The app is published by [your name or the publisher name shown on the App Store]. Contact: [email address].

## What stays on your device

Your steps and progress, your goals and notes, your answers to "How I like to work", your settings, up to 10 chats, your recall answers and parked thoughts. These are stored in the app's own private folder on your device. They are not sent to us. There is no account, no sign-in and no sync.

The app never asks for or stores your name, your age, your school, a diagnosis, your contacts or your location. It never records or stores audio.

## Backups

If "Include in my backup" is on (it starts on), the app's saved data is part of your own iPhone or iPad backup, in iCloud or on your computer. That backup is yours and is managed by Apple. You can switch it off in Me. "Delete everything" clears the data on your device. An older backup keeps its copy until your device makes a new backup.

## Voice

If you turn on Talk and use the microphone button, your voice is turned into text on your device. It is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. The first time, the phone may need to download speech files from Apple. Nothing of yours is sent for that.

## The AI

By default the AI runs on your device (Apple Intelligence, on phones that have it). Messages to it do not leave your device. On a phone without it, the app works without AI.

**If you add your own AI service.** In Me you can add an AI service of your own, with its address and your own key. If you add one and choose it on Talk, the app sends the following to the address you entered, and that service's own privacy rules apply: your chat messages, the step and assignment text the step coach uses, short lines from your own notes and records that you can see and delete, and the app's instructions. The app shows which service your messages go to on the screen where you use it. If the address starts with http:// the messages are not encrypted on the way, and the app tells you. Your key is kept only in your device's Keychain and is sent only to that service.

We do not run any AI service and we never see these messages. Please do not add a service unless a parent or guardian agrees, and read that service's own privacy policy and age rules first.

## What can leave your device when you ask

- **Look it up** sends the words you search for to Wikipedia. Opening a source is a normal visit to that website.
- **Check connection** (for your own AI service) sends one short test message, not your words, and that service's key to the address you entered.
- **Look up models** asks the address you entered for its list of model names, with that service's key. It sends none of your words.

## What we do not do

No analytics. No crash reports. No advertising. No tracking. No code from other companies inside the app. We do not sell or share your information, because we never have it.

## Notes and goals are private

Nothing is sent to your parents, teachers or anyone else. The app does not report on you.

## Children and teenagers

The app is meant for students aged [13 and over / confirm the age you want]. Because the app collects nothing, there is no personal information for us to hold or delete. A parent or guardian is welcome to look at the app with a student. [Confirm this section against the laws that apply where you publish, for example rules about children's privacy.]

## Safety

The app is not a counsellor. If something a student writes suggests they may be in danger or distress, the app shows a short message encouraging them to talk to a trusted adult.

If the app changes what it stores or sends, we will update this page and the date above. The app's About and privacy screen always shows the current list.
