# Checks on a real iPhone

Things automation cannot show. Each task that adds something Ben can try adds numbered steps here: what to open, what to tap or say, what he should see or hear. Ben's report is the test.

Record for each run: the date, the iPhone model, the iOS version, whether Apple Intelligence is on, and what happened at each step.

## First look (T04 to T07)

1. Open the app for the first time. You should see a screen about the app, "Your work stays on this iPhone", and Start. No question, no pop-up asking for permission.
2. Tap Start. You should see Today asking "What's the assignment?" and four places at the bottom: Today, Talk, Plan, Me.
3. Tap Start with nothing typed. You should see a calm line about adding a few words. Nothing else happens.
4. Type "Five paragraphs on courage", pick Essay, tap "Suggest some to start". You should see the steps in an editor, not a step to work on.
5. Change the words of one step. Add "This and that" as a step: you should see "This looks like two steps. Split it?" Tap No: the step stays.
6. Swipe away the app (close it fully) and open it again. You should be back in the editor with your changes.
7. Tap Start. You should see one step, "Step 1 of N", and five buttons the same size. No button is coloured differently.
8. Tap Still working, then Stuck. The Stuck sheet shows a hint. Tap Another hint. Ask a person should appear. Fill one box and tap Copy my question, then paste somewhere (Notes) to check.
9. Tap Drifted. Write a smaller piece and tap Split it. You should see "Good catch." and the smaller piece as the step.
10. Tap Later. You should be on Today. Tap Start this step.
11. Tap each Done until finished. You should see "That's today done." and nothing asking you to do more.
12. Make text bigger (Settings, Display and Brightness, Text Size) and turn on dark mode. Every screen above should stay readable with nothing cut off.
13. Close the app mid-step and open it again later (try the next day). You should see the same step, with "Welcome back." and nothing about how long.

## Look it up (T08)

14. Open Talk, tap Look it up. You should see a line saying your words go to Wikipedia, before you search anything.
15. Type "courage" and tap Search. You should see up to five pages, each a title and one line, with no strange symbols or tags in the text.
16. Tap one. You should see the page, with "Back to results" above it. Tap it to return.
17. From a step, tap Stuck, then Look it up. The box should already have some words from the step. Search, open a page, tap "Back to my step": you should be on the step.
18. Turn on Airplane Mode and search. You should see "You're offline. Try again later." and nothing else.

## Voice in (T09)

Voice is off at first. First turn it on: Me, Voice, "I talk" (arrives in the Me task; until then ask the person helping you to set it).

19. With voice off (Quiet), there is no microphone button anywhere. Turn on "I talk": a microphone button appears beside the assignment box.
20. Tap the microphone for the first time. You should see one sentence about the microphone and two buttons, Okay and Not now. The iPhone's own permission question should not appear until you tap Okay. Tap Okay, allow it. You should see "Ready. Hold the microphone and talk."
21. Hold the microphone and say "Five paragraphs on courage, due Friday." You should see "Listening" while you hold, and words appearing in the box. Let go. The words should stay, and you can fix them by typing.
22. Say a word the app is likely to mishear. Check you can change the words before using them.
23. Tap the microphone once quickly: it should keep listening. Tap again: it should stop. Hold and say nothing, then let go: nothing should change in the box.
24. Listen for a phone call or a timer going off while listening: listening should stop and your words so far should stay.
25. Go to iPhone Settings, find the app, turn the microphone off, come back, press the microphone. You should see a calm line about turning it on in Settings and the typing should still work.
26. Close the app while listening (swipe up to Home). The orange microphone dot should disappear at once.

## Voice out (T10)

Turn on Me, Voice, "I talk" or "We talk" first.

27. Start a step. You should see **Read this step**. With headphones in, tap it: you should hear the step, calmly. The button should say **Stop reading**; tap it and the voice should stop at once.
28. Take the headphones out while it is speaking. The voice should stop and not move to the speaker.
29. With no headphones and "Only speak through headphones" on, tap Read this step. You should hear nothing and see the line about headphones.
30. Turn "Only speak through headphones" off and tap Read this step. You should hear it through the phone. Flip the silent switch: it should go quiet.
31. Turn on Quiet. The Read this step button should be gone.
32. While it is reading, tap Done. The voice should stop.
33. While it is reading, play music in another app, or get a call. Note what happens (it should stop on a call).
