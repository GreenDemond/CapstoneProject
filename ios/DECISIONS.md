## Standing decisions

| # | Decision | Why | Status |

| D5 | The app is fully usable with no AI | Not every phone has the AI, and the no-AI path must always exist | Decided |

### D8. What may leave the phone

| When | What | To |
|---|---|---|
| The student taps Look it up | The look-up words | Wikipedia |
| The AI looks something up (only if the answer-giving checks pass) | The search words the AI chose | Wikipedia |
| The student opens a source | A normal web page visit | That website |
| The student has added an AI service under Me (none at first), has chosen it on Talk, and chats or uses the step coach | The student's chat messages, the step text and assignment text the step coach uses, the app's rules (with the student's AI guide and the kind of work), the model name, the thinking setting (one of none, low, medium, high, sent as reasoning_effort), and that service's own key in the Authorization header. If the service answers 400 and names the setting, the same messages are sent once more to the same service without it | The address of the service the student chose. That service's own privacy rules apply. If the address starts with http:// it is not encrypted |
| The student has chosen their own AI service and taps Ask me a question on Stuck | The kind of work, the step text and the words the student typed in "I tried" (each quoted), and short lines from the student's own record: up to two of their patterns tagged stuck or starting, and one course line for the same class, which holds the kind of work and up to eight of the steps the student kept in an earlier assignment for that class (each quoted; at most five lines and 600 characters in all), the app's fixed rules, the model name, and that service's key in the Authorization header | The address of the service the student chose. With Apple's on-device model nothing leaves. **Parent to confirm before classmates** |
| The student has chosen their own AI service and taps Ask me a question on a step in the editor that looks like two steps | The step text (quoted), the app's fixed rules, the model name, and that service's key in the Authorization header | The address of the service the student chose. With Apple's on-device model nothing leaves. **Parent to confirm before classmates** |
| The student has chosen their own AI service and taps Check my steps against the assignment (S3) | The assignment text (quoted), the app's fixed rules, the model name, the thinking setting (sent as reasoning_effort, as for the step coach), and that service's key in the Authorization header. If the service answers 400 and names the setting, the same messages are sent once more without it. With Apple's on-device model nothing leaves. **Parent to confirm before classmates** | The address of the service the student chose |
| The student taps Check connection on a service | One short fixed test message (no student words), the model name, and that service's key in the Authorization header | The address of the service the student chose |
| The student taps Look up models while adding or editing a service | A request for the list of model names (no student words), with the key just typed or that service's saved key in the Authorization header. The list address is the entered address with /models in place of /chat/completions, on the same host; the app never guesses another | The address of the service the student chose |
| The backup switch is on (it starts on) | The app's saved data. Never the AI key, which stays on this iPhone only | The student's own iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), when the backup switch is on |

**Tailscale exception (parent, 2026-11-21):** an http address on a `*.ts.net` name or in 100.64.0.0/10 does not get the "not encrypted" line on Talk, because a tailnet encrypts it end to end. The tick box when saving any http address still applies. Caveat: 100.64.0.0/10 is also used by internet providers for their own networks, so an address in that range is not proof of Tailscale. Before classmates, decide whether to keep the range or only `*.ts.net` names.

No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or sync. Audio never leaves and is never stored. The AI key is stored only in the Keychain and goes nowhere except in the Authorization header to the address of the service it was saved for. Each key is kept under that service's id, so one service's key is never sent to another service's address.
